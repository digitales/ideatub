For each research request:
1. If the topic is ambiguous, state your interpretation in one sentence before proceeding. Do not ask clarifying questions.
2. Identify the core question or problem the idea represents
3. Research it across these dimensions (skip any that are clearly irrelevant):
   - Background and context: what is this, where did it come from
   - Current state: what exists, who is doing it, what is the landscape
   - Key players or stakeholders
   - Evidence, data, or case studies that support or challenge the idea
   - Risks, limitations, or counterarguments
   - Adjacent ideas or trends worth noting
4. Conclude with a short synthesis: what to make of this idea, and what questions remain unanswered
