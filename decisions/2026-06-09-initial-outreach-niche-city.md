# Decision: Initial outreach niche and city for nthdesigns Prospect Scanner

**Date:** 2026-06-09  
**Project:** nthdesigns  
**Status:** Accepted

---

## Decision

Start with **private dental practices in Birmingham**.

## Why dental

- Single decision-maker (principal dentist or practice manager)
- High transaction value
- Clinicians are poor at managing their own digital presence
- EAA/WCAG urgency is real
- Practices almost always have a website so the full dual-signal audit works
- Hospitality has too many sole traders (PECR risk)
- Legal has slower decision cycles

## Why Birmingham over Manchester

- Larger population means more practices before the niche exhausts
- Manchester has a stronger established agency scene
- "dental practice Birmingham" is a higher-volume search term making the GBP competition gap easier to frame
- The city choice is less important than copy and report quality — Birmingham is a pragmatic start

## First search

- Query: `private dentist Birmingham`
- Scan type: combined
- Target prospects with 10–50 reviews and a website
- Ignore sub-5 reviews with no website

## Next steps

1. Send 20–30 outreach emails
2. Assess response rate over 3–4 weeks
3. Add Manchester as city two if niche is working
4. If response rate is poor, treat as a copy problem not a city problem
